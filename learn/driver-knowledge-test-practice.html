Driver knowledge test practice

Road rules you need for your learner or driver knowledge test, and for changing an overseas licence to an Australian one - with every answer explained.

How the practice works

  • 20 questions each time, with no time limit.
  • The answer and the reason are shown after every question.
  • The questions cover rules that are the same, or nearly the same, in every state and territory.
