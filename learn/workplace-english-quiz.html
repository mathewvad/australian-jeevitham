English at work in Australia

Most Australian workplaces are fairly informal. People use first names, even with senior managers, and requests are polite and gentle - "Could you give me a hand with this when you have a minute?" rather than "Help me now."

Many work words are short forms that you will see on rosters, payslips and emails:

WordMeaning
RDORostered day off
by COBBy the end of the working day
WHSWork health and safety
PPEProtective gear such as gloves, masks and boots
time in lieuTime off instead of pay for extra hours
penalty ratesHigher pay for nights, weekends or public holidays

Is it okay to ask someone to repeat an instruction?

Yes. Asking again is normal and respected in Australian workplaces, and guessing can cause mistakes. You can say: "Sorry, could you explain that again? I want to make sure I get it right."

Where can I check my pay and conditions?

Your pay, leave and penalty rates depend on your award or agreement. The Fair Work Ombudsman has free information and tools to check them.
