Citizenship test practice

Twenty questions in 45 minutes, set out like the real Australian citizenship test, with a new set of questions each time.

How the test works

  • 20 questions in 45 minutes, as in the real test.
  • To pass you need at least 15 right (75%), and all 5 questions on Australian values must be right.
  • You see your result, and the right answers, at the end.
  • These practice questions were written by Australian Jeevitham from the official booklet. They are not the real test questions.
