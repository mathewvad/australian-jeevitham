1. Choose the right TRA program

The Offshore Skills Assessment Program (OSAP) applies to specified occupations and passport countries. It is compulsory for permanent migration applicants in certain licensed trades, including Electrician (General), Electrician (Special Class), Plumber (General), and Air-conditioning and Refrigeration Mechanic.

The Migration Skills Assessment (MSA) may apply where TRA is the assessing authority and OSAP is not mandatory. Use the current TRA program pages rather than choosing from old forum advice.

2. OSAP Pathway 1 and Pathway 2

Pathway 1 is generally for applicants without a relevant Australian VET qualification. Pathway 2 is for applicants who hold a relevant Australian VET qualification or current identified Australian occupational licence.

An approved registered training organisation conducts the assessment. Documentary review may be followed by technical assessment. The format depends on the occupation and pathway, and travel may be required.

3. Build credible Gulf employment evidence

Experience certificate മാത്രം ആശ്രയിക്കരുത്. Employer letter-ൽ dates, hours, duties, tools, projects, supervisor contact എന്നിവ വ്യക്തമാക്കുക. Support it with independent evidence such as payslips, bank salary credits, employment contracts, tax or social-insurance records, residency records and project evidence.

Documents not in English need compliant translations. Never alter a letter to make duties fit an occupation. Inconsistencies can cause delay, verification or refusal.

4. Skills assessment is not a state licence

A positive TRA outcome supports a migration application, but it may not authorise unsupervised licensed work. Electrical, plumbing and refrigeration licensing is controlled by state and territory regulators. You may need gap training, supervised employment or further licensing steps after arrival.

5. Before submitting

  1. Match your daily tasks to the exact occupation.
  2. Confirm the correct program and approved assessment provider.
  3. Read the current applicant guidelines and fee schedule.
  4. Prepare traceable employment evidence and certified translations.
  5. Check Australian licensing separately.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is OSAP compulsory for every trade applicant?

No. It depends on the nominated occupation, passport country and program rules. Some licensed occupations have mandatory OSAP requirements.

What is the difference between OSAP Pathway 1 and Pathway 2?

Pathway 1 generally covers applicants without a relevant Australian VET qualification. Pathway 2 covers applicants with a relevant Australian VET qualification or identified Australian occupational licence.

Will a positive TRA assessment give me an Australian trade licence?

No. Migration skills assessment and state or territory occupational licensing are separate processes.

Can TRA verify Gulf employment?

Yes. Applicants should expect evidence checks and should provide genuine, consistent documents with contactable employers where possible.
