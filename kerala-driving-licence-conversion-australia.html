ഇന്ത്യൻ ഡ്രൈവിംഗ് ലൈസൻസ് ഉള്ള ഒരു വ്യക്തിക്ക് ഓസ്‌ട്രേലിയയിൽ ഡ്രൈവ് ചെയ്യാൻ സാധിക്കുമോ? ഏതെല്ലാം ഘട്ടങ്ങളിലൂടെയാണ് ഓസ്‌ട്രേലിയൻ ലൈസൻസ് കരസ്ഥമാക്കേണ്ടത്, നിങ്ങളുടെ സ്റ്റേറ്റിലെ നിയമങ്ങൾ എന്തൊക്കെയാണ് എന്ന് ഈ ഗൈഡിൽ കൃത്യമായി വായിക്കാം.

A state-by-state guide for Malayali migrants converting an Indian (Kerala RTO) driving licence to an Australian driver licence across VIC, NSW, QLD, WA, SA, TAS, ACT and NT.

Last updated: September 2026 · Written for new arrivals, PR holders, and temporary visa holders in Australia

Road transport regulations vary significantly across Australian states and territories. Always verify current policies directly with your state's transport authority (VicRoads, Service NSW, TMR QLD, Department of Transport WA, or Service SA). ഈ പോസ്റ്റ് വിവരശേഖരണ ആവശ്യങ്ങൾക്കായി മാത്രമുള്ളതാണ്, ഔദ്യോഗിക ഗതാഗത വകുപ്പിന്റെ നിയമങ്ങൾ കൃത്യമായി പരിശോധിക്കുക.

1. ഇന്ത്യൻ ലൈസൻസിന്റെ നിയമപരമായ സാധുത (Non-Recognised Country Rules)

ഓസ്‌ട്രേലിയൻ ട്രാൻസ്പോർട്ട് അതോറിറ്റി (Austroads) വിദേശ രാജ്യങ്ങളുടെ ഡ്രൈവിംഗ് ലൈസൻസുകളെ രണ്ട് പ്രധാന വിഭാഗങ്ങളായി തിരിച്ചിരിക്കുന്നു: 'Recognised Countries' ഉം 'Non-Recognised Countries' ഉം. ഇന്ത്യ Non-Recognised വിഭാഗത്തിൽ പെടുന്നതിനാൽ, കേരള ലൈസൻസ് ഉള്ളവർ നേരിട്ട് ലൈസൻസ് മാറാൻ സാധിക്കില്ല.

Indian licence holders generally cannot use a recognised-country test exemption to obtain an Australian licence. Your overseas driving history can still affect the Australian licence class or stage you receive, but the outcome depends on the jurisdiction, your age, when your overseas licence was first issued, how long you have held it and the local licensing rules. There is no single Australia-wide rule that guarantees a full unrestricted licence after three years.

ആർക്കൊക്കെ വിദേശ ലൈസൻസിൽ താൽക്കാലികമായി ഡ്രൈവ് ചെയ്യാം?

  • Temporary visa holders: The period for which you may drive on a valid overseas licence varies by state or territory. Some jurisdictions impose a time limit after you take up residence, while others allow eligible temporary visitors to continue driving on a valid overseas licence. Check the current rule where you live.
  • Permanent residents and Australian citizens: Conversion deadlines vary by jurisdiction. Once your authority to drive on the overseas licence ends, you must not continue driving unless you hold the appropriate Australian licence or permit. Check the official licensing authority for the deadline that applies to your circumstances.
State Specific Rules

2. State-by-State Conversion Rules & Time Limits

ഓരോ ഓസ്‌ട്രേലിയൻ സ്റ്റേറ്റിലും ലൈസൻസ് മാറ്റുന്നതിനുള്ള സമയപരിധികളും ടെസ്റ്റുകളും വ്യത്യാസപ്പെട്ടിരിക്കുന്നു:

🏛️

Victoria (VicRoads)

Time limit: If you live in Victoria for more than 6 months, you generally need to convert your overseas licence.
Indian licence pathway: Testing can include a knowledge test, hazard perception test and drive test, depending on your circumstances.
Important: If you fail a Victorian drive test, you cannot continue driving on your overseas licence.

🌉

New South Wales (Service NSW)

Time limit: Australian citizens and permanent residents generally need to transfer within 3 months of taking up residence. Temporary overseas visitors who live in NSW for more than 6 months generally need a NSW licence.
Indian licence pathway: Applicants from non-recognised countries generally need to meet NSW knowledge and practical driving-test requirements.
Important: Check Service NSW for the licence class and test sequence that applies to your age and driving history.

☀️

Queensland (TMR)

Time limit: This 3-month conversion deadline applies once you become a permanent resident or Australian citizen. If you're still on a temporary visa, you can generally keep driving on your valid overseas licence — check TMR for your specific situation.
Indian licence pathway: A road-rules test and practical driving test generally apply to non-recognised-country licence transfers.
Important: If you fail the practical driving test, your authority to drive on the overseas licence is withdrawn.

🌾

Western Australia (DoT WA)

Time limit: This 3-month window applies once you hold permanent residency or citizenship. If you're on a temporary visa, you can generally keep driving on your valid overseas licence — check the current DoT WA rule for your visa type.
Indian licence pathway: Non-recognised-country applicants generally need a theory test and practical driving assessment.
Important: Temporary visitors can be subject to different rules, so check the current WA requirements for your visa and residency status.

🍇

South Australia (Service SA)

Time limit: The 3-month deadline applies from the date you become a permanent resident, not from when you first arrive on a temporary visa — temporary visa holders can generally keep driving on their valid overseas licence in the meantime.
Indian licence pathway: Non-recognised-country conversion can involve theory, hazard-perception and practical competency requirements.
Important: Check Service SA for the current assessment pathway and documents required for your licence class.

🌿

Tasmania (Transport Tasmania)

Time limit: Permanent residents generally need to convert within 6 months. Eligible temporary visa holders may continue using a valid overseas licence while the relevant conditions are met.
Indian licence pathway: Non-recognised-country applicants can be required to complete a road-rules knowledge test, hazard perception test and practical driving assessment.
Important: Verify the current Tasmanian requirements before booking assessments.

🏛️

Australian Capital Territory (Access Canberra)

Indian licence pathway: Non-recognised-country applicants are subject to the ACT overseas-licence conversion process, which can include the ACT Pre-Learner Licence Course, Road Rules Knowledge Test and a practical driving assessment.
Important: Confirm the current process with Access Canberra for your licence and residency circumstances.

🌅

Northern Territory (MVR)

Time limit: New NT residents are generally directed to transfer an overseas licence within 3 months.
Indian licence pathway: India is not within the recognised-country exemption pathway, so additional testing or assessment may apply.
Important: Confirm the exact test and evidence requirements with NT Motor Vehicle Registry before applying.

3. ആവശ്യമായ രേഖകൾ (Document Checklist)

സർവീസ് സെന്റർ സന്ദർശിക്കുന്നതിന് മുൻപ് താഴെ പറയുന്ന രേഖകൾ കയ്യിൽ ഉണ്ടെന്ന് ഉറപ്പുവരുത്തുക:

  1. Your original overseas driving licence: Bring your current licence and any additional licence-history evidence requested by the state or territory authority.
  2. English translation, if required: If your licence is not fully in English, provide a translation accepted by the relevant licensing authority. NAATI-accredited translations are commonly accepted, but check the authority's current requirements.
  3. Licence history or verification: A driving licence extract or other official record from the issuing authority may be useful or required where the first issue date, licence class or driving history cannot be established from the licence itself.
  4. Proof of identity and residential address: Bring the identity and address documents required by your state or territory licensing authority.
  5. Visa or residency evidence: Some authorities may require evidence of Australian residency or visa status when determining which overseas-licence rules apply.

4. ലൈസൻസ് മാറ്റുന്നതിനുള്ള ഘട്ടങ്ങൾ (Typical Conversion Process)

The exact conversion process varies by state and territory. The steps below are common stages you may encounter, but your jurisdiction may use a different sequence, omit some stages, or require additional assessments. Always check the official requirements for your state or territory.

Step 1: Check Your Eligibility & Documents

ആദ്യം നിങ്ങൾ താമസിക്കുന്ന സ്റ്റേറ്റ് അല്ലെങ്കിൽ ടെറിട്ടറിയിലെ ഔദ്യോഗിക ലൈസൻസിംഗ് അതോറിറ്റിയുടെ വെബ്സൈറ്റിൽ നിങ്ങളുടെ eligibility, ആവശ്യമായ രേഖകൾ, overseas licence conversion നടപടികൾ എന്നിവ പരിശോധിക്കുക. Prepare your overseas licence, proof of identity and address, and any translation or licence-history documents required by that authority.

Step 2: Road Rules Knowledge Test

നിങ്ങളുടെ സ്റ്റേറ്റ് അല്ലെങ്കിൽ ടെറിട്ടറിയിൽ knowledge test ആവശ്യമാണെങ്കിൽ, അവിടുത്തെ ഔദ്യോഗിക Driver Handbook ഉപയോഗിച്ച് തയ്യാറെടുക്കുക. The test name, format and eligibility requirements vary by jurisdiction. Study the official road rules, including speed limits, give-way rules, roundabouts, alcohol limits and other local requirements.

Step 3: Hazard Perception Test — If Required

ചില സ്റ്റേറ്റുകളിലും ടെറിട്ടറികളിലും Hazard Perception Test (HPT) ആവശ്യമായേക്കാം. Where required, this assessment tests your ability to identify and respond appropriately to developing road hazards. Whether you need an HPT, and when you must complete it, depends on your jurisdiction and licensing pathway.

Step 4: Practical Driving Assessment — If Required

നിങ്ങളുടെ licence conversion pathway-ൽ practical driving test ആവശ്യമാണെങ്കിൽ, Australian road rules അനുസരിച്ച് സുരക്ഷിതമായി വാഹനം ഓടിക്കാനുള്ള കഴിവ് പരിശോധിക്കും. Assessment criteria vary by jurisdiction but can include observation, speed management, signalling, lane positioning, gap selection and compliance with road rules.

Important: Do not assume that completing these four stages automatically results in a full unrestricted licence. The licence you receive depends on the rules of your state or territory and factors such as your age and overseas driving history.

5. ഡ്രൈവിംഗ് ടെസ്റ്റിൽ മലയാളി ഡ്രൈവർമാർ വരുത്തുന്ന പ്രധാന തെറ്റുകൾ (Crucial Test Tips)

ഇന്ത്യയിൽ വർഷങ്ങളുടെ ഡ്രൈവിംഗ് പരിചയമുള്ളവർ പോലും ഓസ്‌ട്രേലിയൻ റോഡ് ടെസ്റ്റിൽ പരാജയപ്പെടാറുണ്ട്. ഇതിന് പ്രധാന കാരണം ഇന്ത്യയിലെ ഡ്രൈവിംഗ് ശീലങ്ങളും ഓസ്‌ട്രേലിയയിലെ കർശനമായ റോഡ് നിയമങ്ങളും തമ്മിലുള്ള വ്യത്യാസമാണ്.

  • Observation and Blind-Spot Checks: Use mirrors and appropriate shoulder/blind-spot checks before manoeuvres such as changing lanes or merging. Unsafe or inadequate observation can result in test errors or failure under the applicable assessment criteria.
  • Roundabout Rules: Give way to vehicles already on the roundabout — not simply vehicles approaching from your right. Use the correct lane and indicators, and indicate left when exiting where required and practicable under your state or territory road rules.
  • Speed Management: Obey the posted speed limit and adjust your speed to the conditions. Speeding can result in assessment errors or test termination depending on the jurisdiction and circumstances. Pay particular attention to school-zone limits and operating times.
  • Hook Turns (Melbourne CBD): In Victoria, right turns at specific city intersections are conducted from the far-left lane (Hook turn). Practice this if taking your test near the Melbourne city boundary.
  • Professional Lessons: Consider lessons with a qualified local driving instructor to become familiar with Australian road rules, observation standards and test expectations. Do not rely on claims about fixed or guaranteed test routes.
FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

The questions Malayali drivers ask most often about converting a Kerala licence.

Can I directly swap my Kerala driving licence for an Australian licence?

A direct test-free transfer is generally not available for an Indian licence. The assessments required depend on the state or territory and can include knowledge, hazard-perception and practical driving tests. Check the current conversion pathway where you live.

What happens if I fail the practical driving test in Australia?

The consequences differ between states and territories. In some jurisdictions, including Queensland and Victoria, failing the practical driving test can end your authority to continue driving on your overseas licence. You may then need a learner licence or permit and must comply with learner conditions before attempting another test. Check the specific rule in your jurisdiction before booking the practical assessment.

Do I get a Full Licence or a Provisional (P-Plate) Licence?

It depends on the state or territory, your age, how long you have held your overseas licence, when it was first issued, and the applicable local licensing rules. Overseas driving experience may be taken into account, but there is no single Australia-wide rule that guarantees a full unrestricted licence simply because you have held an Indian licence for three years.

Is an International Driving Permit (IDP) from India required?

An International Driving Permit (IDP) does not replace your underlying overseas licence and is not a substitute for converting your licence when local rules require conversion. Translation requirements differ by jurisdiction, so check which translations or supporting documents your licensing authority accepts.
