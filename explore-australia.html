Travel Ideas

A few places to start your research

🐠

Great Barrier Reef

The Great Barrier Reef stretches along Queensland's east coast and can be explored from regions including Cairns, Port Douglas, Townsville, the Whitsundays and the Southern Great Barrier Reef.

🌿

Daintree & Tropical North Queensland

Rainforest, reef and Aboriginal cultural experiences make Tropical North Queensland one of Australia's most distinctive travel regions. Cairns and Port Douglas are common bases for exploring the area.

🪨

Uluru & the Red Centre

Uluru-Kata Tjuta National Park is one of Australia's best-known cultural and natural destinations. Visitors should respect the continuing cultural significance of the area to Anangu Traditional Owners.

🏙️

Australian Cities

Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Perth, Adelaide, Hobart, Darwin and Canberra each offer very different climates, food, culture and day-trip options.

🚗

Road Trips & Regional Australia

Australia's long distances can make road trips rewarding but demanding. Check driving times, fuel availability, weather, road closures and mobile coverage before travelling remotely.

🏖️

Beaches, Islands & National Parks

Australia's coastlines, islands and national parks vary greatly by season and region. Check official park, marine and local tourism information for access, permits and safety notices.

Plan for Australia's size and changing conditions

Distances that look small on a map can involve many hours of driving or a flight. Weather also varies dramatically between tropical northern Australia, the southern states, alpine regions and the interior.

Before travelling, check current weather forecasts, road conditions, park or marine closures, bushfire or cyclone warnings where relevant, and any permit requirements. For remote trips, plan fuel, water and communications carefully.

Queensland: reef, rainforest and regional travel

Queensland is especially strong for reef, rainforest, island and tropical travel. Tourism Australia describes the Great Barrier Reef as the world's largest coral reef system, while Tropical North Queensland combines access to both the Great Barrier Reef and the Daintree Rainforest.

If you are starting from Queensland, the official Queensland tourism website is useful for regional itineraries, events and destination planning.

Use official destination information before booking
