സോഷ്യൽ വർക്കർമാർക്കുള്ള AASW സ്കിൽസ് അസസ്‌മെന്റ് ഗൈഡ് 2026

ഓസ്‌ട്രേലിയയിലേക്ക് കുടിയേറാൻ ആഗ്രഹിക്കുന്ന സോഷ്യൽ വർക്ക് യോഗ്യതയുള്ളവർക്കായി — AASW-യുടെ ഔദ്യോഗിക രേഖകൾ മാത്രം അടിസ്ഥാനമാക്കി തയ്യാറാക്കിയ, വസ്തുതാപരമായി പരിശോധിച്ച ഗൈഡ്.

A fact-checked guide to the AASW Migration & Eligibility Assessment (MEA), built strictly from AASW's official published criteria, fees, and English language policy — not assumptions or agent claims.

Research completed: September 2026 · Sourced directly from aasw.asn.au official pages — verify current details before applying

1,000 hrs MINIMUM FIELD EDUCATION REQUIRED

കുറഞ്ഞത് 2 പ്ലേസ്മെന്റുകളിലായി 1,000 മണിക്കൂർ ഫീൽഡ് എജ്യുക്കേഷൻ വേണം; ഒന്ന് ഡയറക്ട് പ്രാക്ടീസിൽ ആയിരിക്കണം. Across at least two placements with two contrasting practice foci — one must be direct practice.

1. എന്താണ് AASW? (What is AASW?)

Australian Association of Social Workers (AASW) ആണ് ഓസ്‌ട്രേലിയൻ ഗവൺമെന്റ് അംഗീകരിച്ച, സോഷ്യൽ വർക്ക് തൊഴിൽ വിഭാഗത്തിനുള്ള ഔദ്യോഗിക "assessing authority". മൈഗ്രേഷനുവേണ്ടിയുള്ള നിങ്ങളുടെ വിദേശ സോഷ്യൽ വർക്ക് യോഗ്യത വിലയിരുത്തുന്നത് ഇവരാണ്.

AASW is the Australian Government's nominated assessing authority for the Social Worker occupation. It runs the Migration & Eligibility Assessment (MEA), which checks whether an overseas social work qualification is comparable to an AASW-accredited Australian one, under the Australian Social Work Education and Accreditation Standards (ASWEAS).

ℹ️

Important distinction: A positive AASW migration assessment is not the same thing as AASW membership, and is not the same as legal registration to practise. AASW also separately assesses eligibility for AASW membership (relevant to employment), which is a related but distinct process from the migration assessment.

ടിപ്പ്: മൈഗ്രേഷൻ അസസ്‌മെന്റ് പാസായത് കൊണ്ട് മാത്രം AASW അംഗത്വമോ, ജോലി ചെയ്യാനുള്ള അനുമതിയോ സ്വയമേവ ലഭിക്കില്ല.

2. AASW-യുടെ 5 യോഗ്യതാ മാനദണ്ഡങ്ങൾ (The 5 Official Eligibility Criteria)

AASW-യുടെ ഔദ്യോഗിക വെബ്സൈറ്റിൽ പറയുന്ന അഞ്ച് മാനദണ്ഡങ്ങൾ എല്ലാം ഒരുപോലെ പാലിക്കണം:

  1. Specific social work qualification: The qualification must be a specific qualification in social work — not a related field like psychology, sociology, or general community services.
  2. Recognised as professional in the country of training: The qualification must be regarded as a professional social work qualification in the country where it was obtained.
  3. Comparable learning outcomes: The learning outcomes must be comparable to what graduates of AASW-accredited Australian social work programs demonstrate.
  4. 1,000 hours of field education: At least 1,000 hours of field education, across a minimum of two placements with two contrasting practice foci, and at least one placement must be in direct practice.
  5. High-level English proficiency: As specified in the AASW English Language Policy (covered in detail below).
📌

This requirement recently increased: AASW's field education minimum was raised from 980 hours to 1,000 hours to align with current AASW-accredited course standards. If you've seen "980 hours" quoted elsewhere (including in some third-party guides), that figure is outdated.

3. ഇംഗ്ലീഷ് ഭാഷാ ആവശ്യകത (English Language Policy — Effective 20 May 2026)

ഇത് വളരെ പ്രധാനപ്പെട്ട ഒരു കാര്യമാണ്: AASW നിലവിൽ IELTS Academic ടെസ്റ്റ് മാത്രമേ അംഗീകരിക്കുന്നുള്ളൂ. PTE, OET, TOEFL എന്നിവ AASW സ്വീകരിക്കുന്നില്ല — ഇവ വിസ ആവശ്യങ്ങൾക്ക് സ്വീകാര്യമായേക്കാമെങ്കിലും AASW അസസ്‌മെന്റിന് അല്ല.

AASW currently accepts only the IELTS Academic test (plus the IELTS Academic One Skill Retake, in specific circumstances) — not PTE, OET, or TOEFL. This is different from the Department of Home Affairs' visa English requirements, which do accept a broader range of tests. Applicants must meet one of three options:

  • Option 1: Overall 7.0 in one sitting, with a minimum of 7.0 in all four components (listening, reading, writing, speaking).
  • Option 2: A minimum of 7.0 in each of the four components, achieved across a maximum of two sittings within a 6-month period, with no single component below 6.5.
  • Option 3: One full IELTS Academic sitting with 7.0 in three of four components (none below 6.5), plus one IELTS Academic One Skill Retake to bring the remaining component up to 7.0, taken within 60 days of the original test.

Test results must be no more than 3 years old at the time of your AASW application.

Exemption from English testing

ചില അപേക്ഷകർക്ക് ഇംഗ്ലീഷ് ടെസ്റ്റ് ഒഴിവാക്കാം. You may be exempt if you completed all of Years 7–12 taught and assessed in English in Australia, Canada, NZ, Ireland, South Africa, the UK, or the USA, and completed at least 3 years of full-time tertiary study (Bachelor's or higher) taught and assessed in English in one of those same countries. Both conditions must be met — completing only your degree in English, without also completing full secondary schooling in one of those countries, does not qualify for the exemption.

4. ഫീസും പ്രോസസ്സിംഗ് സമയവും (Current Fees & Processing Times — FY2025–26)

2025 ജൂലൈ 1 മുതൽ പ്രാബല്യത്തിലുള്ള ഔദ്യോഗിക ഫീസ് നിരക്കുകൾ താഴെ:

Assessment Type Outside Australia Within Australia (incl. GST)
Migration & Eligibility Assessment (main qualification assessment) AU$2,123.64 AU$2,336.00
Skilled Employment Assessment (1–3 positions, optional) AU$943.64 AU$1,038.00
Skilled Employment — each additional position (4+) AU$176.36 AU$194.00
Doctorate Degree Assessment (optional) AU$530.91 AU$584.00

Source: AASW official fee schedule, effective 1 July 2025 (FY2025–26). Fees are reviewed periodically — always confirm the current figure on AASW's Migration & Eligibility Assessment Fees page before paying.

⏱️

Processing time: 8–10 weeks from the date your application is marked "pending assessment" (i.e. once your documents are complete and ready for an assessor — not from the date you first submit). If you've added the optional skilled employment assessment, expect it to take longer than the standard 8–10 weeks.

അപേക്ഷ പൂർണ്ണമായി "pending assessment" ആയ തീയതി മുതലാണ് 8-10 ആഴ്ച എണ്ണുന്നത് — അപേക്ഷ സമർപ്പിച്ച തീയതി മുതലല്ല.

5. വിസ പാതകൾ (Occupation Status & Visa Pathways)

Social Worker (ANZSCO 272511) ഓസ്‌ട്രേലിയയുടെ Medium and Long-term Strategic Skills List (MLTSSL)-ൽ ഉൾപ്പെടുന്നു. എന്നാൽ ഇത് ഓട്ടോമാറ്റിക് ആയി ഒരു വിസ ഗ്യാരണ്ടി ചെയ്യുന്നില്ല.

Social Worker (ANZSCO 272511) sits on the Medium and Long-term Strategic Skills List (MLTSSL), which makes it potentially eligible across several pathways, including Subclass 189 (points-tested, no sponsorship), 190 (state/territory nominated), and 491 (regional provisional). It's also referenced for the 485 Temporary Graduate visa and certain employer-sponsored options (482, 186), subject to their own separate criteria.

⚠️

Occupation eligibility ≠ visa eligibility ≠ an invitation. Being on MLTSSL means the occupation can be nominated — it does not mean you personally will receive an invitation. Points cut-offs for Subclass 189/190/491 change with every invitation round and are set by demand, not by a fixed rule. We are not going to quote a specific "points you need" figure here, because any number would be out of date within weeks. Check current rounds directly on the Department of Home Affairs' SkillSelect page before planning around any number you've seen elsewhere, including on this page.

ഒരു ഒക്യുപേഷൻ ലിസ്റ്റിൽ ഉള്ളത് കൊണ്ട് മാത്രം വിസ ലഭിക്കില്ല. പോയിന്റ് കട്ട്ഓഫുകൾ ഓരോ റൗണ്ടിലും മാറും — ഏറ്റവും പുതിയ വിവരങ്ങൾക്കായി Home Affairs-ന്റെ ഔദ്യോഗിക വെബ്സൈറ്റ് പരിശോധിക്കുക.

State & territory nomination

Several states have historically included Social Worker on their nominated occupation lists for Subclass 190 and/or 491, but each state sets its own criteria, quotas, and residence/work requirements — and these change every program year. Rather than list state-by-state details here (which would likely be outdated by the time you read this), check each state's current skilled migration page directly: NSW, Victoria, Queensland, South Australia, Western Australia, and others via their individual state migration portals.

6. കേരളത്തിൽ നിന്നുള്ള അപേക്ഷകർക്ക് (Practical Notes for Kerala/India-Qualified Applicants)

AASW ഒരു പ്രത്യേക കേരള/ഇന്ത്യൻ യൂണിവേഴ്സിറ്റി ലിസ്റ്റ് പ്രസിദ്ധീകരിക്കുന്നില്ല. ഒരു യൂണിവേഴ്സിറ്റിയുടെ പേര് മാത്രം വച്ച് അസസ്‌മെന്റ് പാസാകുമെന്ന് ഉറപ്പ് പറയാൻ കഴിയില്ല.

🛑

We could not find an AASW-published list naming specific Indian or Kerala universities/colleges as automatically accepted or rejected. Acceptance cannot be guaranteed based solely on the institution name — AASW assesses each application against the five criteria above (qualification type, professional recognition, comparable learning outcomes, 1,000 hours of field education, and English). A BSW or MSW from any Indian university still has to demonstrate it meets these criteria individually, particularly the field education hours and structure.

Common document issues from India

  • Consolidated vs semester-wise marksheets: Some Kerala universities issue a single consolidated marksheet; others issue separate semester marksheets. Check what AASW's application guide requires and provide whichever documents your university actually issues, certified as required.
  • Field placement evidence: Since AASW's 1,000-hour requirement is specific (two placements, two contrasting foci, one in direct practice), a general "completion certificate" from your college may not be enough — you may need placement-specific documentation showing hours, dates, supervisor details, and practice type.
  • Medium of Instruction letters: A "Medium of Instruction: English" letter from your university is not the same as meeting AASW's English language policy. AASW's English requirement is met only via IELTS Academic (or the specific secondary+tertiary education exemption described above) — a MOI letter alone does not satisfy it.
  • Name differences: If your name is spelled differently across your passport, degree certificate, and marksheets (common with transliteration from Malayalam), be prepared to provide supporting evidence or a statutory declaration linking the documents.
Fact Check

7. സോഷ്യൽ വർക്ക് മൈഗ്രേഷൻ മിഥ്യകൾ (Myths — Fact Checked Against Official Sources)

Claim Verdict Explanation
"PTE is accepted for the AASW assessment." FALSE AASW currently accepts only IELTS Academic (plus the One Skill Retake in specific cases). PTE, OET, and TOEFL are not accepted for the migration assessment, even though some of these are accepted for visa purposes by Home Affairs.
"The field education requirement is 980 hours." OUTDATED This was true previously but AASW increased the minimum to 1,000 hours to align with current AASW-accredited course standards.
"AASW assessment guarantees PR." FALSE A positive AASW assessment is one requirement among several. It does not guarantee an EOI invitation, state nomination, or visa grant — those depend on points, quotas, and demand at the time you apply.
"If your occupation is on the list (MLTSSL), you will get an invitation." FALSE Being on the occupation list means the occupation is eligible to be nominated — invitations depend on your points score relative to that round's competition, which changes constantly.
"You must have work experience (e.g., 3 years) to pass the AASW skills assessment." FALSE Post-qualifying work experience is 100% optional for AASW. To get a positive migration assessment, you only need the qualification, the 1,000 hours of academic field placement, and IELTS 7.0. You can apply as a fresh graduate with zero work experience.
"Any Social Work degree from India is accepted." FALSE AASW assesses every qualification individually against its five criteria — institution name alone doesn't determine the outcome.
"A Medium of Instruction (English) letter satisfies the English requirement." FALSE AASW's English requirement is met via IELTS Academic testing, or a specific secondary-plus-tertiary education exemption — not simply by a university confirming classes were taught in English.
"AASW membership is the same as being registered to practise." FALSE Social work in Australia does not have the same statutory registration model as, for example, nursing (AHPRA). AASW membership and a migration skills assessment are related but distinct from any employer's own registration or hiring requirements.

Verdicts are based on AASW's own published criteria and English Language Policy as of September 2026. If AASW updates its policy, some of these may change — always check the source links in this guide.

8. നിങ്ങളുടെ സാഹചര്യം വിലയിരുത്താൻ എന്തൊക്കെ വേണം? (What Information Is Needed to Assess Your Situation)

നിങ്ങളുടെ വ്യക്തിഗത യോഗ്യത കണക്കാക്കാൻ, താഴെ പറയുന്ന വിവരങ്ങൾ ആദ്യം ആവശ്യമാണ് — ഇവയില്ലാതെ ആർക്കും കൃത്യമായ ഉത്തരം നൽകാൻ കഴിയില്ല:

  • Your qualification (BSW/MSW/other), university, mode of study (regular/distance), and graduation year
  • Total field placement hours completed, number of placements, and their practice focus
  • Current English test scores (if any), or which of the exemption pathways might apply to you
  • Total years of post-qualifying social work employment, and where (India/overseas/Australia)
  • Your age, citizenship, and current visa status (if already in Australia)
  • Your preferred state/territory and visa subclass, if you have one in mind

This page cannot calculate your personal eligibility — that requires an AASW assessment of your actual documents, and for visa/points questions, either the Department of Home Affairs' own tools or a MARA-registered migration agent.
