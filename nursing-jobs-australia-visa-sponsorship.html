Who Sponsors Nurses

Health services that publish their nurse sponsorship rules

These health employers say on their own websites that they can sponsor internationally qualified nurses or midwives. Each one has its own conditions, which are summarised below from their official pages. Public health services

Queensland

Townsville Hospital and Health Service

  • You do not need to be an Australian citizen or permanent resident to apply.
  • Lists "Assistance with Visa processes" and help with gaining registration among its benefits.
  • Relocation help may include the visa fee for you and your partner, the Ahpra application fee, the NMBA assessment fee for internationally qualified nurses and midwives, up to $3,000 per family for removal costs, economy flights and up to six weeks of accommodation.
  • Contact: NMWorkforce@health.qld.gov.au
Townsville international nursing page →
New South Wales

Western NSW Local Health District

  • Can support sponsorship nominations for the Skilled Employer Sponsored Regional (Provisional) visa (subclass 494).
  • You must have at least 3 years of relevant work experience in your nominated occupation, with statements of service.
  • You must have a positive ANMAC skills assessment.
  • New rural generalist nurses do two weeks of training at the CREST centre in Wellington, NSW before starting.
Western NSW international candidates page →
Victoria

The Royal Melbourne Hospital

  • May offer temporary or permanent visas, depending on the nurse's experience, qualifications and registration.
  • Depending on the visa, you need one to three years of full-time post-qualification experience in the occupation.
RMH international recruitment page →
Western Australia

WA Health

  • Can help with sponsorship for temporary or permanent visas, but currently only for experienced nurses and midwives applying for positions in areas of critical shortage.
  • Steps: register with the Nursing and Midwifery Board of Australia, apply for a job, then negotiate sponsorship with the hospital or health service after you are offered the job.
  • Enrolled nurses are generally not eligible for sponsorship with WA Health.
  • WA Health does not sponsor visas for bridging courses, English tests or other study.
WA Health international nurses page →
South Australia

SA Health

  • If you meet the eligibility criteria, SA Health says it will sponsor your visa and help you through the visa process.
  • SA Health is an approved sponsor for the subclass 482 and subclass 494 visas.
  • Its rough guide: registration takes about 6-10 weeks after an official job offer, and the visa about 5-11 months.

NT Health

Australia-wide

Opal HealthCare

  • Can sponsor eligible nurses on the subclass 482 visa, with possible permanent residency later through the subclass 186 visa.

Before You Apply

What to sort out before you apply

🩺

1. Registration comes first

To work as a nurse or midwife in Australia you need registration with the Nursing and Midwifery Board of Australia (NMBA) through Ahpra. Several health services above ask you to start registration before you apply. Registration ഇല്ലാതെ sponsorship കിട്ടാൻ സാധ്യത കുറവാണ്.

Read our AHPRA nursing registration guide →

🛂

2. Know the visa names

Employer-sponsored visas you will see in nursing ads:

  • Skills in Demand visa (subclass 482) - temporary. Some ads still call it by its old name, "TSS".
  • Skilled Employer Sponsored Regional (subclass 494) - provisional, for regional areas.
  • Employer Nomination Scheme (subclass 186) - permanent.

A visa usually also needs a skills assessment from ANMAC, which is separate from Ahpra registration.

🚨

3. Never pay for a sponsored job

Under Australian law, a sponsor must not ask for or take money or any other benefit in return for sponsoring you, and you must not offer one. Paying the government's visa application fee for your own visa is normal; paying anyone to get the job or the sponsorship is not. Be careful with anyone who asks for a "placement fee" for a sponsored nursing job. Every ad on this page is applied for online through the employer's own website.
