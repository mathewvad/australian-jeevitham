1. നഴ്‌സിംഗ് രജിസ്‌ട്രേഷൻ പാത്ത്‌വേകൾ (AHPRA / NMBA Pathways)

ഓസ്‌ട്രേലിയയിൽ നഴ്‌സിംഗ് രജിസ്‌ട്രേഷൻ നൽകുന്നത് Nursing and Midwifery Board of Australia (NMBA) ആണ്; Ahpra അപേക്ഷയും രജിസ്‌ട്രേഷൻ പ്രക്രിയയും നടത്തുന്നു. Since 23 April 2025, the NMBA has had an additional registration standard that can streamline registration for eligible internationally qualified registered nurses (IQRNs) who have already registered and practised in approved comparable international jurisdictions. Applicants who do not meet that standard continue through the existing IQNM assessment process.

A. Streamlined IQRN Registration Pathways - eligible applicants may avoid OBA exams

എല്ലാ വിദേശ നഴ്‌സുമാർക്കും ഈ streamlined pathway ലഭിക്കില്ല. നിങ്ങളുടെ qualification, registration history, clinical practice, comparable jurisdiction എന്നിവ NMBAയുടെ മാനദണ്ഡങ്ങൾ പാലിക്കണം.

  • Comparable jurisdiction: You must meet the NMBA requirements relating to registration and RN practice in an approved comparable international regulatory jurisdiction.
  • Clinical practice: The streamlined IQRN standard recognises experienced RNs who have completed at least 1,800 hours of practice since 1 January 2017 in an NMBA-approved comparable jurisdiction.
  • Approved comparable jurisdictions: NMBA material identifies jurisdictions including the United Kingdom, Republic of Ireland, United States, Singapore, Spain, and the Canadian provinces of British Columbia and Ontario. Eligibility depends on the detailed pathway requirements, not simply on having worked in one of these places.
  • Other registration standards: All applicants must still meet applicable NMBA registration standards, including English language skills, recency of practice, criminal history and professional indemnity insurance arrangements.

For a nurse who trained and worked only in India/Kerala, a streamlined pathway should not be assumed. Complete Ahpra's Self-check and follow the pathway assigned to your qualification and registration history. Do not rely on a simple rule such as “degree after 2017 = fast track”.

B. Existing IQNM Assessment Process - including Stream B (OBA)

Streamlined IQRN pathway-ന്റെ മാനദണ്ഡങ്ങൾ പാലിക്കാത്തവർ existing IQNM assessment process വഴിയാണ് പോകുന്നത്. Self-check, qualification assessment എന്നിവയുടെ അടിസ്ഥാനത്തിലാണ് നിങ്ങളുടെ stream തീരുമാനിക്കുന്നത്.

  • Stream B: Applicants assessed into Stream B complete the Outcomes-Based Assessment (OBA), which for registered nurses includes the NCLEX-RN multiple-choice examination and the NMBA OSCE.
  • India/Kerala applicants: Many nurses applying with an India-trained qualification and without a qualifying streamlined-registration history may be directed through the existing IQNM process. The actual outcome is determined by Ahpra/NMBA assessment, not by nationality alone.
  • Stream C: Applicants whose qualification does not meet the required qualification criteria may need further education or qualification upgrading before becoming eligible for registration.

For RN registration, the NMBA assesses whether the applicant's nursing qualification meets the relevant qualification criteria, including the required academic level. Always rely on the Self-check and subsequent Ahpra assessment for your individual pathway.

2. Stream B (OBA) രജിസ്‌ട്രേഷൻ പ്രക്രിയ - Typical Step-by-Step Process

  1. Self-Check, IQNM Account & Orientation Part 1
    Ahpra Self-check പൂർത്തിയാക്കി നിങ്ങളുടെ qualification വിവരങ്ങൾ നൽകുക. ആവശ്യമായ cohort-ൽ ആണെങ്കിൽ non-refundable IQNM assessment and orientation fee ആയ AUD 410 അടച്ച് Orientation Part 1 പൂർത്തിയാക്കണം. After completing Self-check and creating your IQNM account, you have 90 days to complete Orientation Part 1. You must complete it to progress further in the IQNM assessment process.
  2. Portfolio / Qualification Assessment & Verification
    Ahpra ആവശ്യപ്പെടുന്ന passport/identity, nursing qualification, academic records, registration history, employment or practice evidence, registration verification / Certificate of Good Standing എന്നിവ നിർദ്ദേശിച്ച രീതിയിൽ സമർപ്പിക്കുക. The exact documents depend on your assessment pathway. Follow the document and verification instructions shown in your Ahpra/IQNM account rather than using a generic checklist.
  3. NCLEX-RN Multiple-Choice Examination
    Stream B Registered Nurse applicants-ക്ക് MCQ examination ആയി NCLEX-RN ഉപയോഗിക്കുന്നു. Once you are eligible, registration and scheduling are handled through the NCLEX/Pearson VUE process. NCLEX-RN is delivered at authorised test centres in many countries, so applicants may be able to sit this stage outside Australia.
  4. NMBA OSCE - Adelaide or Melbourne
    NCLEX-RN requirement പൂർത്തിയാക്കി OSCE-യ്ക്ക് eligible ആയ ശേഷം, NMBAയുടെ നിർദ്ദേശപ്രകാരം പരീക്ഷ ബുക്ക് ചെയ്യാം. The RN OSCE is conducted in person in Australia at Adelaide Health Simulation in Adelaide or the RANZCOG assessment centre in Melbourne. The current OSCE fee is AUD 4,000 per sitting, so applicants should also budget for travel, visa and accommodation costs.
  5. Apply for / Complete Registration Requirements
    പരീക്ഷകളും assessment requirements-ഉം പൂർത്തിയാക്കിയതിന് ശേഷം Ahpra/NMBA നിർദ്ദേശിക്കുന്ന registration application നടപടികൾ പൂർത്തിയാക്കുക. You must satisfy all applicable registration standards, including English language skills, recency of practice, criminal history and professional indemnity insurance arrangements. After registration, IQNMs must complete Orientation Part 2 within six months.

The exact order and documentation can vary depending on the outcome of Self-check and qualification assessment. The steps above describe the common Stream B RN journey, not a guarantee that every applicant will follow an identical sequence.

4. ന്യൂസിലാൻഡ് പാത്ത്‌വേ (NZ Registration / TTMRA)

ന്യൂസിലാൻഡിൽ രജിസ്റ്റർ ചെയ്ത നഴ്‌സുമാർക്ക് Trans-Tasman Mutual Recognition arrangements പ്രകാരം ഓസ്‌ട്രേലിയൻ രജിസ്‌ട്രേഷൻ സംബന്ധിച്ച വേറിട്ട മാർഗ്ഗം ഉണ്ടായേക്കാം. എന്നാൽ ഇന്ത്യയിൽ നിന്ന് ആദ്യം ന്യൂസിലാൻഡ് രജിസ്‌ട്രേഷൻ നേടി ശേഷം ഓസ്‌ട്രേലിയയിലേക്ക് മാറുന്നത് എല്ലാവർക്കും ലളിതമായ “shortcut” ആണെന്ന് കരുതരുത്.

The Nursing Council of New Zealand now uses a competence-assessment process for internationally qualified nurses where required, including theoretical assessment and an in-person OSCE in Christchurch for candidates directed to that process. This can add another country's registration, examination, travel and fee requirements before an Australian application is considered.

അതുകൊണ്ട് നിങ്ങളുടെ ലക്ഷ്യം ഓസ്‌ട്രേലിയൻ രജിസ്‌ട്രേഷൻ മാത്രമാണെങ്കിൽ, Ahpra/NMBA Self-check വഴി നിങ്ങളുടെ നേരിട്ടുള്ള Australian pathway ആദ്യം പരിശോധിക്കുക. Nurses who already hold New Zealand registration should check the current TTMRA/Australian registration requirements that apply to them.

5. ജോലിയും വിസയും (Jobs & Visas for Australia)

ഓസ്‌ട്രേലിയയിൽ Registered Nurse ആയി ജോലി ചെയ്യാൻ സാധാരണയായി NMBA registration ആവശ്യമാണ്. Registration improves your ability to apply for nursing roles, but it does not guarantee employment, sponsorship or a visa.

ANMAC Skills Assessment: ANMAC conducts migration skills assessments for nurses and midwives where a skills assessment is required for the relevant visa or migration pathway. Nurses who already hold current Australian or New Zealand nursing registration may be eligible for ANMAC's Modified Skills Assessment. ANMAC assessment is for migration purposes; it does not itself authorise you to practise nursing in Australia.

പ്രധാന വിസ ഉദാഹരണങ്ങൾ:

  • Subclass 482 - Skills in Demand (SID): a temporary employer-sponsored skilled visa. Sponsorship, occupation, work-experience and other eligibility requirements apply.
  • Subclass 190 - Skilled Nominated: a permanent skilled visa requiring state or territory nomination, an invitation to apply, and satisfaction of the relevant skilled-migration criteria.
  • Subclass 491 - Skilled Work Regional (Provisional): a provisional regional visa available through state/territory nomination or eligible family sponsorship. It can provide a pathway toward permanent residence when the relevant requirements are met.
  • Subclass 186 - Employer Nomination Scheme (ENS): a permanent employer-sponsored visa with different streams and eligibility requirements.

Visa rules, occupation lists, nomination criteria and employer requirements can change independently of nursing registration rules. Check the Department of Home Affairs and the relevant state or territory migration authority, or obtain advice from a registered migration agent. See our migration pathways guide for general information.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions Kerala and India-trained nurses ask about Australian nursing registration.

Am I automatically eligible for a streamlined AHPRA/NMBA pathway if I trained in India?

No. The streamlined IQRN registration standard is for eligible registered nurses who meet the NMBA's detailed requirements relating to registration and practice in approved comparable international jurisdictions, including at least 1,800 hours of RN practice since 1 January 2017. An India/Kerala qualification or a post-2017 graduation date by itself does not guarantee streamlined eligibility.

Do I need to sit NCLEX-RN and OSCE if I apply directly from Kerala?

Do not assume the outcome before completing Self-check. Many India-trained RN applicants who do not meet a streamlined pathway are assessed through the existing IQNM process. Applicants placed in Stream B complete the OBA, which includes the NCLEX-RN MCQ examination and the NMBA OSCE.

Where is the Australian RN OSCE held?

The NMBA RN OSCE is delivered in person in Australia at Adelaide Health Simulation in Adelaide, South Australia, and at the RANZCOG assessment centre in Melbourne, Victoria. Candidates must travel to Australia for the OSCE.

Is the New Zealand route automatically a shortcut into Australian nursing registration?

No. New Zealand has its own registration and competence-assessment requirements for internationally qualified nurses, including an in-person OSCE in Christchurch for candidates directed to that process. Nurses who already hold New Zealand registration should check the current Australian TTMRA requirements, but using New Zealand only as an intermediate step can add cost and time.

What's the difference between Ahpra/NMBA and ANMAC?

The NMBA sets the registration requirements for nurses and Ahpra administers the registration process. ANMAC conducts migration skills assessments where required for relevant migration pathways. An ANMAC assessment does not itself authorise a person to practise nursing in Australia.

Can I combine two English test attempts?

Potentially, yes. National Boards can accept results from a maximum of two sittings within a 12-month period, but each accepted test has specific minimum floor scores for the individual sittings. Results from different test providers cannot be combined. Check Ahpra's current score table for your test and test dates.

What English scores apply if I test in 2026?

Ahpra changed the minimum accepted English-test scores from 23 April 2026. Tests taken on or after that date use the current score table, while tests taken on or before 22 April 2026 may fall under the transition arrangements. Check the official Ahpra accepted-English-tests page before booking or relying on a result.

How much does the Stream B process cost?

Key current official fees include the AUD 410 IQNM assessment and orientation fee for relevant cohorts, USD 200 NCLEX registration plus a USD 150 international scheduling fee where applicable, and AUD 4,000 per RN OSCE sitting. Registration, English testing, travel, accommodation, translation, migration assessment and visa costs are additional.
