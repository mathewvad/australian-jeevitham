Privacy policy.

What this website collects, why, and what you can do about it - in plain English. ഈ വെബ്സൈറ്റ് എന്തെല്ലാം വിവരങ്ങൾ ശേഖരിക്കുന്നു, എന്തിന്, നിങ്ങൾക്ക് എന്ത് ചെയ്യാം എന്ന് ലളിതമായി.

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Who we are

This website, australianjeevitham.com, is run by Binu Vadakkedath (Australian Jeevitham) in Queensland, Australia. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Australian Jeevitham. If you have a question about your privacy, please use our Contact page.

The short version

  • We do not sell or rent your information, and there are no advertising networks on this website.
  • We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are useful.
  • If you send us feedback, we receive what you type in the feedback box.
  • The calculators, tests and tech tools work inside your own browser. What you type in them is not sent to us.
  • The English Coach app has its own privacy policy, because it has accounts and an AI coach.

Visitor statistics (Google Analytics)

Every page uses Google Analytics. It tells us how many people visit, which pages they read, roughly which country or city they are in, what kind of device and browser they use, and how they found the site (for example from YouTube or a Google search). Google Analytics uses cookies to do this. We see totals and trends, not your name.

Google processes this information under the Google Privacy Policy. You can read how Google uses information from sites that use its services. To stop Google Analytics, block cookies in your browser or install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

Feedback boxes

Many pages have a "Was this page useful?" box, and the English Coach app has a Feedback page. When you use them, we receive:

  • your Yes or No answer, and your message, if you write one
  • your name, email address or WhatsApp number, only if you choose to give them
  • the name of the page you were on

This goes to our small server on Cloudflare, where it is saved, and messages are emailed to Binu using a service called Resend. We use it only to reply to you and to improve the website.

To stop spam, the server counts how many messages arrive from each internet (IP) address each day. These counts are deleted after 90 days. Please do not put passwords, bank details or other private information in a message.

What is saved on your own phone or computer

Some pages remember things for you, using your browser's own storage. This information stays on your device. We cannot see it.

  • Money calculators remember the numbers you entered, so you do not have to type them again. Tap Clear my numbers on the Money page to remove them.
  • Tests and quizzes remember your best score.

You can remove all of it at any time by clearing your browser's data for this website.

Tools that run in your browser

The Money calculators, Tests and quizzes and Tech tools do their work inside your browser. Text you paste, files you choose and answers you give are not uploaded to us. Two tech tools (the QR code generator and the JSON, CSV and YAML converter) load a small piece of code from cdnjs, a Cloudflare service, the same way most websites load code.

Other services that load on our pages

  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts on our pages, so your browser contacts Google to download them.
  • YouTube videos on our pages use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode. YouTube may still set cookies once you play a video.
  • Links to YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Threads and other websites take you to services with their own privacy policies.

The English Coach app

The English Coach app works differently: it has accounts, an AI coach and pronunciation scoring. Its own privacy policy explains what it collects and why. Read the English Coach privacy policy.

Where your information is stored

The services we use (Google, Cloudflare and Resend) are run by international companies and may store information in Australia or in other countries.

Your choices

You can ask us what feedback we hold from you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Please use our Contact page and tell us roughly when you sent it and from which page. For general information about privacy in Australia, see the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect, we will update this page and the date at the top.
