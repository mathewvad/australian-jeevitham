Living with Queensland wildlife

In Queensland we share backyards, parks, roads and farms with native animals. Kangaroos, wallabies and magpies are protected under Queensland's Nature Conservation Act 1992, so never harm them, feed them or disturb their nests. Most of the time they are easy neighbours - it helps to know when they are most active.

Times of year to know

WhatWhen
Magpie swoopingMostly August to November, when there are chicks in the nest
Kangaroos and wallabies on the roadAll year, most of all at dawn, at dusk and at night
Hand-pollinating passion fruitIn the morning, when the flowers are fully open

Who to call

  • A magpie swooping in a park, street or school - tell your local council, so they can put up warning signs. For advice, call the Queensland Government on 13 QGOV (13 74 68).
  • An injured, sick or orphaned kangaroo or wallaby - contact RSPCA Queensland.
  • Someone harming or illegally feeding kangaroos - call 1300 130 372.

The Queensland Government has more advice on living with magpies and living with kangaroos.
