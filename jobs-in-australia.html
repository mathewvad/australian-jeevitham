Use SEEK and Workforce Australia for broad job searches, and use the state and territory government career portals for public-sector vacancies. A job advertisement does not automatically mean visa sponsorship is available, so check the advertisement carefully for work-right, citizenship/residency, registration, licensing and sponsorship requirements before applying.

Applying from Kerala or Overseas

ഇന്ത്യയിൽ നിന്നോ ഗൾഫിൽ നിന്നോ apply ചെയ്യുമ്പോൾ advertisement-ൽ overseas applicants, work rights, registration, licence, sponsorship എന്നിവയെ കുറിച്ച് എന്താണ് പറയുന്നതെന്ന് ശ്രദ്ധിക്കുക. Do not assume that every vacancy offers employer sponsorship. If sponsorship is important to you, look for explicit wording in the advertisement or contact the employer/recruiter directly before spending money on relocation or third-party services.

Know Your Workplace Rights

Employees working in Australia have minimum workplace entitlements. For most employees, minimum pay and conditions come from an award or registered agreement; award/agreement-free employees remain entitled to the National Employment Standards and the National Minimum Wage. Employees on probation still retain their applicable minimum entitlements.

Watch for Job Scams

Be cautious if a supposed employer or recruiter asks you to pay money upfront to secure a job, contacts you unexpectedly through messaging apps, promises unusually high income for little work, or asks for sensitive identity or banking information before you have verified the organisation. Scamwatch specifically warns against job offers that require you to pay money to make money.

Read Scamwatch job-scam guidance →

Are there “jobs in demand” in Australia?

Jobs and Skills Australia publishes the Occupation Shortage List (OSL), which reports occupations assessed as being in shortage nationally or in particular states and territories. It is a point-in-time labour-market assessment, not a guarantee that an individual will find a job, receive sponsorship, qualify for a visa, or meet professional registration requirements.
