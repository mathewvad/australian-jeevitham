1. Resume എഴുതുന്നതിന് മുമ്പ് job advertisement വായിക്കുക

Resume format തിരഞ്ഞെടുക്കുന്നതിന് മുമ്പ് employer എന്താണ് ആവശ്യപ്പെടുന്നത് എന്ന് വേർതിരിച്ചെടുക്കുക: essential criteria, desirable criteria, licence, registration, work rights, location, shifts, closing date, file type, page limit എന്നിവ.

Make a short evidence map before writing. Put each important requirement in one column and one genuine example from your experience beside it. This prevents a common problem: a polished resume that never answers what the employer is actually looking for.

ATS-Friendly Formatting

2. Keep the document easy for people and systems to read

ATS എന്നത് ഒരു magic keyword machine അല്ല. Employers വ്യത്യസ്ത സസോഫ്റ്റുവെയറുകളും screening methods-ഉം ഉപയോഗിക്കുന്നു. അതുകൊണ്ട് universal guarantee ആർക്കും നൽകാനാവില്ല.

📄

Use a simple reading order

Use one main column, familiar section headings and a clear reverse-chronological employment history. ഏറ്റവും പുതിയ ജോലിയുടെ ഡീറ്റെയിൽസ് ആയിരിക്കണം ആദ്യം കാണിക്കേണ്ടത്. Avoid placing essential information only inside text boxes, graphics, icons, headers or footers.

🔎

Use relevant wording honestly

Use important role language from the advertisement where it accurately describes your experience. Workforce Australia recommends using relevant keywords across the application, but copying a list without evidence will not strengthen it.

📎

Follow the requested format

If the portal asks for Word, PDF, a short pitch, a page limit or separate criteria responses, follow that instruction. Check the final file opens properly and has a professional filename.

Conservative ATS checklist

  • Use standard headings such as Summary, Skills, Employment History, Education and Registrations.
  • Use readable fonts, consistent dates and ordinary bullet points.
  • Write abbreviations with the full term at first mention where useful, such as Australian Health Practitioner Regulation Agency (Ahpra).
  • Do not use keyword stuffing, invisible text or false job titles.
  • Use a filename such as FirstName-LastName-Resume-Registered-Nurse.pdf, adapted to your own name and role.
  • Proofread the text after exporting. A visually correct document can still contain broken or missing searchable text.
Resume Structure

3. What to include in an Australian resume

നിങ്ങളുടെ മുഴുവൻ ജീവിതചരിത്രമല്ല resume. ഈ role-ന് നിങ്ങളെ interview ചെയ്യേണ്ടതിന്റെ കാരണം വേഗത്തിൽ കാണിക്കുന്ന relevant record ആണ്.

  1. Name and contact details: Use a professional email address, reachable phone number and your city/state or current location. A full street address is normally unnecessary at application stage.
  2. Professional summary: Use three or four specific lines covering your role, relevant experience, strongest capability and the type of opportunity you are targeting.
  3. Key skills: Select skills that genuinely match the advertisement. Avoid vague lists such as "hard-working" without evidence.
  4. Employment history: Show employer, location, job title and dates. Add concise responsibilities, then emphasise outcomes, scale, improvements or problems solved.
  5. Education, licences and registration: List qualifications and current professional requirements relevant to the job. State assessment or registration status accurately.
  6. Systems and tools: Include software, clinical systems, machinery or technical tools only where relevant.
  7. Referees: Follow the advertisement. Ask permission before listing someone and confirm their title, phone and email details.

Turn duties into evidence

Weak: Responsible for customer enquiries.

Stronger: Managed customer enquiries across phone and email, resolved escalations and maintained accurate case notes within service timeframes.

Use numbers only when they are true. A credible result without a percentage is better than an invented "30% improvement".

🪪

Personal information: include only what the application genuinely needs.

Indian റെസ്യുമുകളിൽ സാധാരണ കാണുന്ന photo, date of birth, marital status, religion, passport number, Aadhaar number തുടങ്ങിയ വിവരങ്ങൾ Australian resume-ൽ സാധാരണയായി ആവശ്യമില്ല. Employer വ്യക്തമായി ആവശ്യപ്പെടാത്ത sensitive identity documents ആദ്യ application-ൽ അയക്കരുത്.

If work rights are relevant, describe your status accurately and briefly. Do not upload identity or banking documents to an unverified employer or recruiter.

Cover Letter

4. Write a short letter that connects you to this role

Queensland Government guidance recommends a tailored one-page cover letter that introduces you, identifies the position, highlights your strongest qualities and encourages the reader to continue to your resume. It should not simply repeat the resume.

Company name മാത്രം മാറ്റിയ generic letter recruiters-ന് എളുപ്പം തിരിച്ചറിയാം. എന്തുകൊണ്ട് ഈ role, എന്തുകൊണ്ട് ഈ organisation, എന്ത് relevant evidence നിങ്ങൾക്കുണ്ട് എന്നീ മൂന്ന് കാര്യങ്ങൾ വ്യക്തമായി പറയുക.

  1. Opening: Name the role and explain why it genuinely interests you.
  2. Evidence: Match two or three priority requirements with brief examples.
  3. Organisation fit: Refer to a real service, value, project or need relevant to the employer.
  4. Close: Confirm your interest, availability and preferred contact details without sounding entitled to an interview.

Natural opening example

I am applying for the Support Officer position advertised on SmartJobs. My experience coordinating customer enquiries, maintaining accurate records and supporting time-sensitive operational work aligns closely with the role requirements. I am particularly interested in the position because of the team's focus on accessible public services.

Do not copy this word for word. Replace it with your own role, evidence and reason.

Selection Criteria

5. Answer the criteria with evidence, not adjectives

Government, health, education, council, university, community-sector applications-ൽ selection criteria, capabilities, role requirements അല്ലെങ്കിൽ short pitch ചോദിക്കാം. ചില employers separate document ആവശ്യപ്പെടും; ചിലർ cover letter-ൽ address ചെയ്യാൻ പറയും.

Read the instructions carefully. The Australian Public Service may ask for a short pitch rather than separate responses, while other employers may require each criterion to be addressed. The requested format and word limit always take priority.

Use STAR to organise a real example

  • Situation: What was happening?
  • Task: What were you responsible for?
  • Action: What did you personally do, and why?
  • Result: What changed, what was delivered, or what did you learn?

Short STAR example

Criterion: Ability to manage competing priorities.

During a month-end reporting cycle, two urgent client data issues arrived while scheduled reports were due. I assessed the business impact, agreed priorities with my manager, documented each issue and separated immediate fixes from follow-up work. I delivered the required reports on time and resolved both client issues without losing the audit trail.

The example works because it shows context, judgement, action and result. "I have excellent time-management skills" does not provide the same evidence.

Applying From Overseas

6. Help the employer understand your situation quickly

🌏

Translate context, not experience

Keep genuine overseas job titles, but briefly explain unfamiliar employers, systems or responsibility levels. Do not rename yourself as "Manager" or "Registered Nurse" if that was not your actual role or current registration status.

🛂

Be accurate about work rights

If asked, state your current work-right or sponsorship position clearly. Do not imply that registration, a skills assessment or visa sponsorship is complete when it is still pending.

📞

Make contact practical

Use a monitored email address, include your current location and time zone where relevant, and provide an Australian number only if you genuinely have one. Confirm interview availability without hiding that you are overseas.

Professional Outreach

7. Contact people professionally, without asking for shortcuts

Recruiter-നോ contact officer-നോ message അയയ്ക്കുന്നത് തെറ്റല്ല. പക്ഷേ "job തരാമോ?", repeated follow-up, unrelated CV forwarding എന്നിവയ്ക്ക് പകരം ഒരു specific, answerable question ചോദിക്കുക.

Before applying

Subject: Question about Support Officer vacancy - Ref QLD/12345

Hello Ms Taylor, I am preparing an application for the Support Officer vacancy. Could you please confirm whether the requested two-page statement should address each capability separately or as one integrated response? I have reviewed the advertisement and role description but could not find this detail. Thank you.

After applying

Hello Mr Chen, I submitted my application for the Systems Analyst position on 12 September. I remain very interested in the role and am available if the panel needs any further information. I understand the recruitment process may take time, so no immediate response is expected.

One courteous follow-up may be reasonable when no recruitment timeline was provided. Repeated calls or messages rarely improve an application.

🤖

Using AI responsibly in a job application

AI can help you organise notes, shorten sentences, check grammar or compare your draft with the advertisement. You remain responsible for every claim. Never let a tool invent achievements, qualifications, registrations, employers, dates or selection-criteria examples. Read the final application aloud and rewrite anything that does not sound like you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Australian resume and cover letter FAQ

Should I include a photograph in an Australian resume?

A photograph is not normally required unless the advertisement specifically requests one for a legitimate reason. Use the space to show relevant skills, experience and achievements instead.

How long should an Australian resume be?

There is no universal page limit. Keep the resume concise and relevant to the role. Follow any length or document instructions in the advertisement, as these take priority.

Should I use the same resume for every job?

No. Keep a master resume, then tailor the summary, skills and evidence for each genuine vacancy without changing or exaggerating the underlying facts.

Do all Australian jobs require selection criteria?

No. Selection criteria or capability responses are more common in government, health, education and community-sector recruitment. Follow the application instructions for each vacancy.

Should I submit my resume as PDF or Word?

Use the file type requested by the employer or recruitment system. If no format is specified, confirm that your chosen file opens correctly and preserves a clean, readable layout.
