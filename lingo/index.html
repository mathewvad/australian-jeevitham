How to use these pages

Good English at work in Australia comes from three things: knowing the words, understanding how people use them, and speaking often. These pages help with each step:

  • Learn the words - the Aussie slang list has 62 everyday words and the workplace English list has 42 work words, each with a meaning and an example sentence.
  • Check yourself - try the Aussie slang quiz and the workplace English quiz, with every answer explained.
  • Speak - practise real work conversations with the English Coach app, which listens and gives you feedback.
  • Read - see why good English matters at work, and get your resume and cover letter right.

Will these pages get me ready for IELTS, PTE or OET?

They help with the everyday and workplace English that every test and job needs. For a visa or registration test, also practise with the official materials for your test, and check the score you need with Home Affairs or your registration board.

Tips that really help

  • Speak a little every day - even ten minutes of talking helps more than an hour of only reading.
  • Learn words inside whole sentences, so you know how to use them.
  • When you do not understand something at work, ask: "Sorry, could you say that again?" Australians are happy to repeat.
