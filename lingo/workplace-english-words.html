Lingo / Workplace English Words

Workplace English words

Words and phrases you hear at work in Australia, about pay, leave, shifts, safety and meetings - each with its meaning and an example.

42 words, A to Z.

a heads-up
An early warningThanks for the heads-up about the meeting.
ABN
Business number used by contractors and companiesYou need an ABN to work as a contractor.
action item
Task someone must do after a meetingYour action item is to call the supplier.
annual leave
Paid holiday time from workI am taking two weeks of annual leave in December.
ASAP
As soon as possiblePlease reply ASAP.
award
Legal document that sets the minimum pay and conditions for an industryNurses are paid under the Nurses Award.
by COB
By the end of the working dayPlease send the report by COB Friday.
call in sick
Phone work to say you are too ill to come inHe called in sick this morning.
casual
Worker paid by the hour, with no paid leaveI started as a casual and became permanent later.
cover a shift
Work a shift in place of a co-workerCan you cover my shift on Saturday?
cover letter
Letter sent with your résumé that says why you suit the jobWrite a new cover letter for each job.
escalate
Pass a problem to someone more seniorIf the customer is still unhappy, escalate it to your manager.
handover
Passing on information to the next shiftThe night nurse gave handover at seven.
incident report
Written record of an accident or near missFill in an incident report after any fall.
induction
Training when you start at a new workplaceYour induction is on your first day.
KPI
Target used to measure how well you do your jobOne of my KPIs is to answer emails within a day.
long service leave
Extra paid leave after many years with one employerAfter ten years she took long service leave.
loop me in
Keep me informedLoop me in on any changes.
minutes
Written record of a meetingCan you take the minutes today?
notice period
Time you must keep working after you resignMy notice period is four weeks.
on call
Ready to come to work if you are neededI am on call this weekend.
overtime
Extra hours worked on top of your normal hoursWe did three hours of overtime last night.
payslip
Record of your pay, tax and super for a pay periodKeep every payslip for your tax return.
penalty rates
Higher pay for nights, weekends or public holidaysSunday shifts pay penalty rates.
performance review
Meeting about how well you are doing your jobMy performance review is in June.
PPE
Protective gear such as gloves, masks and bootsYou must wear PPE in the workshop.
probation
Trial period at the start of a new jobThe first six months are on probation.
RDO
Rostered day offI have an RDO next Monday.
referee
Person who can tell an employer about your workList two referees on your résumé.
roster
Plan that shows who works which shiftsThe new roster comes out on Friday.
run it past someone
Ask for their opinion before you go aheadRun it past your manager first.
salary sacrifice
Paying for something, such as extra super, from your pay before taxHe salary sacrifices into his super.
sick leave
Paid time off when you are illShe is on sick leave today.
sign off
Give final approvalThe supervisor must sign off the timesheet.
super
Money your employer pays into your retirement fundCheck that your super is paid every quarter.
take it offline
Talk about it later, outside this meetingGood point - let’s take it offline.
TFN
Your personal number for taxGive your TFN to your new employer.
time in lieu
Time off instead of pay for extra hoursI worked Saturday, so I will take time in lieu.
toolbox talk
Short safety meeting before work startsWe have a toolbox talk every morning on site.
touch base
Have a short talk to catch upLet’s touch base after lunch.
WFH
Working from homeI am WFH on Fridays.
WHS
Work health and safetyReport any WHS problem to your supervisor.
