Aussie slang words

Everyday Australian words and sayings you will hear at work, at the shops and at school pick-up - each with its meaning and an example.

62 words, A to Z.

ambo
ParamedicThe ambos arrived in five minutes.
arvo
AfternoonSee you this arvo.
bail
Cancel a plan, or leave earlySorry, I have to bail on dinner tonight.
barbie
BarbecueCome over for a barbie on Sunday.
bikkie
BiscuitHave a bikkie with your tea.
bludger
Lazy person who avoids workDon’t be a bludger - give us a hand.
bottle-o
Shop that sells alcoholHe went to the bottle-o on the way to the party.
brekkie
BreakfastLet’s get brekkie before work.
bring a plate
Bring some food to shareThe party is on Saturday - please bring a plate.
BYO
Bring your own drinksThe restaurant is BYO.
chippy
CarpenterThe chippy is building the deck.
chockers
Completely fullThe train was chockers this morning.
chook
ChickenWe keep three chooks in the backyard.
chuck a sickie
Take a day off work when you are not really sickHe chucked a sickie to go fishing.
chuck a U-ey
Make a U-turnWe missed the turn - chuck a U-ey.
crook
Sick or unwellI’m feeling a bit crook today.
cuppa
Cup of tea or coffeeCome in for a cuppa.
dear
ExpensivePetrol is too dear this week.
dob in
Report someone for doing the wrong thingSomeone dobbed him in to the boss.
doona
Quilt for a bedIt’s cold - get the winter doona out.
dunny
ToiletThe dunny is out the back.
esky
Cooler box for food and drinksPut the drinks in the esky.
fair dinkum
True or genuineIs that fair dinkum?
firie
FirefighterThe firies put out the bushfire.
flat out
Very busyI have been flat out all week.
footy
Football - Australian rules or rugby leagueAre you watching the footy tonight?
fortnight
Two weeksI get paid every fortnight.
garbo
Rubbish collectorThe garbos come on Tuesday morning.
good on ya
Well doneYou passed your test? Good on ya!
hard yakka
Hard workPicking fruit all day is hard yakka.
heaps
A lotThanks heaps for your help.
jumper
Sweater or pulloverTake a jumper - it gets cold at night.
keen
Eager or interestedAre you keen for a swim?
knock off
Finish work for the dayWhat time do you knock off?
lollies
Sweets or candyThe kids got a bag of lollies.
Macca’s
McDonald’sLet’s get Macca’s for lunch.
mozzie
MosquitoThe mozzies are bad tonight.
my shout
It is my turn to payPut your wallet away, it’s my shout.
no worries
No problem, or you are welcomeThanks for the lift! - No worries.
op shop
Shop that sells second-hand goods for a charityI bought this jacket at the op shop.
postie
Person who delivers the mailHas the postie been yet?
reckon
Think or believeI reckon it will rain today.
rego
Vehicle registrationMy rego is due next month.

RelativesThe rellies are coming for Christmas.
ripper
ExcellentThat was a ripper of a game.
runners
Sports shoesWear your runners for the walk.
servo
Petrol stationI need to stop at the servo for fuel.

It will be okayDon’t worry about the rain - she’ll be right.
smoko
Short break from workWe will finish this after smoko.
snag
SausageThrow a few snags on the barbecue.
sparky
ElectricianCall a sparky to fix the wiring.
stoked
Very happyShe was stoked to get the job.
sunnies
SunglassesDon’t forget your sunnies.
ta
Thank youTa for the coffee.
thongs
Rubber sandals (flip-flops)Wear your thongs to the beach.
togs
SwimwearBring your togs - there is a pool.

Yes, I can do that without any troubleCan you send it today? - Too easy.
trackies
Tracksuit pantsI wear trackies at home.
tradie
Tradesperson, such as a plumber or builderThe tradies start work at six.

Vehicle with an open tray at the backLoad the tools in the ute.
