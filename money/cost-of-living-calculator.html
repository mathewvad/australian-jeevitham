Your weekly budget

The rent check uses a common guide: rent above 30% of your pay before tax is called rent stress.

Median weekly rent by city

CityHouseUnitPay needed for a unit

"Pay needed" is the yearly household pay before tax that keeps the unit rent at 30% of pay. Median means the middle rent: half of homes cost more and half cost less. Townsville and Cairns show the rent for a 3-bedroom house and a 2-bedroom unit, from new leases signed in July to September 2026.
